The Death Spiral

UNC Kenan-Flagler Business School · Strategic Cost Accounting

Interactive Widget
Internal Telecom Department. A shared service department provides telephone lines to internal users. Fixed costs are sunk — they don't change when users leave. But the rate charged per line rises as users defect, giving remaining users an incentive to defect too. Adjust the sliders and watch the spiral unfold.

📞 Telecom Department Parameters

Annual Fixed Costs $600,000
Infrastructure, staff, lease — doesn't change when users leave
Variable Cost per Line $200
Per-line cost (maintenance, support)
Starting Users (Lines) 2,000
Number of internal users at the start
Defection Rate per Round 50%
% of users who switch to outside suppliers each period when rate rises
Cost Recovery Formula
Rate = (Fixed Costs ÷ Users) + Variable Cost
Each period: rate rises → more users defect → fixed costs spread over fewer users → rate rises again → ☠️

📊 Spiral Progression

Unit Cost vs. Selling Price (Year 1 rate) —
—
Adjust sliders to begin
Death Spiral Stages
1 Fixed costs spread over starting users
2 Rate charged to recover full cost
3 Users find outside option cheaper
4 Users defect → fewer users remain
5 Fixed costs now spread over fewer users → rate rises
6 Remaining users defect → service collapses ☠️
💡 "The phones still ring. The bills still come. The users are just… somewhere else now."
